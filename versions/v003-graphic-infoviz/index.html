It is a credit co-pilot for New Mexico productions.

It reads spend, explains eligibility in plain language, predicts reimbursement, and builds the documentation trail while the production is still moving.

Show budget-office detail

The system should classify direct production and postproduction spend, NM vendor status, resident labor, NRCE lines, SLO/direct hire talent, QPF activity, certain-area uplift spend, postproduction services performed in New Mexico, and documentation status against NMFO/TRD source material.

The value comes from putting spend into the right official buckets.

RPD-41418 starts with 25% lines for eligible direct production and postproduction expenditures, then separately asks for eligible 5%, 10%, 15%, and 20% categories.

Show deeper incentive logic

The form includes a 5% line for TV pilots/series, a 5% line for qualified production facilities, a 10% line for certain areas of the state, a 15% line for qualifying nonresident industry crew, and a 20% line for expenditures tied to a federal New Markets Tax Credit. The QPF and TV pilot/series 5% credits are alternatives, not additive to each other for the same bucket.

It flags problems when they are still fixable.

Examples: missing QPF support, certain-area mileage/location backup, nonresident BTL outreach proof, SLO workflow gaps, and post-production spend that may not qualify.

Show example evidence prompts

For a flagged line, the product should ask for RPD-41418 support such as account ledgers in Excel, Film Office approval memo, NM Film Office NRCE approval, CPA report if required, SLO/direct hire talent support, postproduction support, and uplift support for QPF, TV pilots/series, or certain-area/rural claims.

Locations make the product feel good for the whole state.

When residents and businesses list film-ready properties, the incentive story becomes local income, not just a tax-credit spreadsheet.

Show location-marketplace detail

Listings should gather practical production details: photos, access, parking, power, noise, nearby support spaces, owner restrictions, availability, insurance expectations, and whether the space helps production document local spend.

Source library

Keep citations visible but calm. This builds trust without forcing every reader into legal detail immediately.
