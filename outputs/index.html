Cited FAQ For Budget Offices

Source-backed copy blocks
What is this product optimizing?

It optimizes New Mexico-qualified production and post-production spend, predicted refundable credit recovery, uplift opportunities, audit readiness, and submission timing. New Mexico TRD describes the credit as applying to film production companies with direct production and direct post-production expenditures subject to New Mexico taxation.

Where do QPF uplifts fit?

NMFO states an additional 5% credit may be available for qualifying use of qualified production facilities, and that the uplift applies only to production time at the facility, not offices attached to the QPF. Source: NMFO Qualified Production Facilities.

How does NRCE differ from SLO?

NRCE concerns eligible nonresident below-the-line crew wages. The SLO desk handles nonresident performer and loan-out workflow separately so cast/talent treatment is not mixed into BTL crew calculations. Source: NMFO NRCE.

What is the wrap documentation clock?

NMFO lists a 60-day window after the last eligible New Mexico expenditure for completion notice, final vendor list, final crew list, and stats form, plus a one-year window for the tax credit application package. Source: NMFO After Wrap.

How is this different from California?

California Program 4.0 is an allocation-window program with jobs-ratio ranking and fiscal-year funding categories. This tool is deliberately hyper-local to New Mexico spend categories, residency rules, vendor qualification, QPF/rural uplift strategy, and NMFO/TRD documentation. Source: California Film Commission Program 4.0 Basics.
