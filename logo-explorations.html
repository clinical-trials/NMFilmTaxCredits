Tiny marks with local soul.

Ten crisp icon directions for NM CreditOps AI. Each one is built from hard-edged shapes so it can become a favicon, app icon, pitch-deck mark, or embroidered production-office sticker without going blurry.

The Pixel Adobe “NM”

My first shortlist from this sheet: 02 Tiny Film Hacienda for heart, 01 Pixel Adobe NM for brand coherence, 06 Location Pin House for community buy-in, and 09 Trusty Audit Buddy if we want the “people fall in love with the tiny icon” route. A strong final could hybridize 02 + 01: a tiny adobe mark whose roofline hints at film and whose windows form an “NM” grid.
